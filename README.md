# 🍕 Pizza Sales Data Analysis Using SQL

## 📌 Project Overview

Analyzed pizza restaurant sales data using **SQL** to uncover customer ordering patterns, sales performance, menu popularity, and product-level insights.

The project focuses on transforming raw pizza sales data into meaningful business insights by using SQL queries to calculate key performance indicators (KPIs), identify popular products, analyze customer ordering behavior, and evaluate product-level sales performance.

This project demonstrates my ability to use SQL for **data exploration, business analysis, KPI calculation, aggregation, filtering, sorting, and extracting actionable insights from transactional data.**

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze pizza sales data and answer important business questions related to:

* Overall sales performance
* Order volume
* Revenue generation
* Product pricing
* Customer ordering preferences
* Pizza size popularity
* Menu item performance
* Most frequently ordered pizza types
* Product-level sales trends

The analysis can help a pizza business understand customer preferences and identify products that contribute most to overall sales.

---

## 🗂️ Dataset

The dataset contains pizza restaurant sales information that can be analyzed to understand:

* Customer orders
* Pizza types
* Pizza sizes
* Pizza prices
* Quantity ordered
* Sales/revenue
* Order-level transactions
* Product-level performance

The data was analyzed using SQL to extract meaningful patterns and business metrics.

# 🔍 Business Questions

The following questions were answered through SQL analysis:

### 1. What is the total number of orders?

Calculated the overall number of customer orders to understand the restaurant's order volume.

---

### 2. What is the total revenue generated from pizza sales?

Calculated total revenue to evaluate the overall sales performance of the business.

---

### 3. What is the highest-priced pizza?

Identified the pizza with the highest selling price to understand the premium products available on the menu.

---

### 4. What is the most common pizza size ordered?

Analyzed order quantities by pizza size to determine customer preferences and identify the most frequently purchased size.

---

### 5. What are the Top 5 most ordered pizza types?

Ranked pizza types based on order quantity to identify the most popular products on the menu.
